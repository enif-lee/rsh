# Busy sample intervals and screen waits: functional evidence

A controller-selected screen wait can overlap the denominator interval of
an actual committed busy-rate sample. Diagnostic 54 records that coexistence
directly. **It does not prove the sample is wrong or should be discarded.**
This is 16 functional-valid jobs/four blocks with 24 trace endpoints and
**performance_eligible=False**, not another FPS comparison or selection win.

The production reference is CAN+PTY `4f98` and the candidate combined `295f`;
traced builds are `d742268da4df` / `3031e105b1aa`. No new capacity policy,
threshold, history clear, margin or user option is introduced. The
[source/build proof](data/busy-span-functional-20261101/source-proof.json)
and [schema](data/busy-span-functional-20261101/SCHEMA.md) describe one added
RAM record site; [main](data/busy-span-functional-20261101/main.patch) and
[combined](data/busy-span-functional-20261101/combined.patch) patches are exact. Source/body joins and functional integrity come from the
frozen analysis; the new independent work reparses sample/poll CSV metadata,
not a new terminal/GPU oracle. Host context was accepted, but no quiet gate
or functional-to-performance promotion follows.

## Strict intersection, not inferred physical idle

A hold requires exact screen-only timer mask 8 (ties separate), a future
positive wait matching the preceding screen choice's effective due, and a
poll returning rc 0/network-revents 0/other-ready 0. The interval is
`[actual enter,min(actual return,due)]`; overshoot and later lock time are
excluded. Same-generation intervals are unioned before intersecting the
sample's `[span_from,passed ACK now]`. A poll timeout is not a claim that no
kernel/network work occurred. Missing/zero intersection does not prove no
controller wait, because eventful/tied/past contexts are excluded.

Record kind 40 `busy_sample_commit` is different from screen-choice branch 40.
It is emitted after append/prune and conditional median assignment, before
rate 25 and later probe/refloor logic. The payload's new capacity is that
stage's value, not necessarily ACK-exit capacity. Decode signed f64 bits
without JavaScript Number truncation. Its interval endpoints are passed
Instant values, not row time or kernel arrival. Record 39 uses actual-entry
column **d** separately from passed-decision column **c**.

## A physical queue can coexist with the hold

A constructed counterexample assumes 446 actual bytes remain at a 64 kbit/s
bottleneck: serialization alone takes 55.75 ms. Sender may already have handed
all bytes to TCP (`all_sent=True`) and place a future screen deadline 30 ms
away, while those old bytes still drain. A busy ACK sample and controller
poll can overlap legitimately. Blanket removal of such low samples could
retain stale high capacity after a fast→slow change and overestimate window,
BDP/drain allowance and screen sending. This is a counterexample to a discard
rule, **not a measurement of diagnostic54's physical queue**.

The evidence motivated a separate one-line freshness A/B before adding
threshold/history/app-limited machinery. It does not establish that A/B's
performance, spare capacity or safe promotion; older adverse predicates and
other cohorts remain separate. No new product policy is justified by 54 alone.
